# Design direction

## Core identity

**Metro Trains** is a Minecraft mod centered on Russian and Soviet-inspired metro systems: rolling stock, stations, tunnels, track infrastructure, passenger facilities, and operational details.

## Train visual style

Use a mixed visual language:
- Realistic overall proportions, recognizable bogies, doors, windows, headlights, and underframe equipment.
- Minecraft-friendly geometry and readable silhouettes at normal gameplay distance.
- Textures should preserve the block-based visual identity of Minecraft rather than aim for photorealism.
- Interior details should be visible and useful without making the models excessively complex.

## Metro inspiration

The first content set should draw from Russian and Soviet metro design:
- Deep underground stations, monumental halls, columns, arches, and durable finishes.
- Familiar Russian-language signage, line colors, station name boards, and wayfinding.
- Metro rolling stock with multi-car formations, side doors, destination displays, and recognizable front ends.
- Infrastructure such as rails, power equipment, signals, platforms, service rooms, and tunnel sections.

This is an artistic direction, not a commitment to reproduce one specific real-world operator or train model.

## Gameplay priorities

1. Build a reliable track and vehicle foundation.
2. Make trains move smoothly and stop accurately at platforms.
3. Support multi-car consists and passenger access.
4. Make stations useful through doors, routes, signs, and passenger interactions.
5. Expand the visual library with station architecture and train variants.
